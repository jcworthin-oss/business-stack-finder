Payroll and people

Payroll software without unnecessary complexity

Compare payroll systems using employee count, state filings, contractor payments, benefits, time tracking, and support needs.

Payroll software is a recurring operational commitment. Evaluate the complete monthly cost and the provider's responsibility for filings—not merely the advertised base price.

Information to gather first

  • Number of employees and contractors
  • States where employees work
  • Pay frequency and hourly versus salaried workers
  • Time tracking, benefits, workers’ compensation, and retirement needs
  • Whether an accountant requires a particular integration

Shortlist framework

NeedPrioritizeVerify
Very small teamSimple onboarding and transparent per-person pricingMinimum monthly charges
Hourly workforceTime tracking, overtime rules, and approvalsWhether time tools are included
Multi-state teamState registrations and filing supportFees for each additional state
Growing benefitsIntegrated benefits and employee self-serviceBroker and carrier availability

Where Gusto may fit

Gusto is often evaluated by small businesses seeking payroll, tax workflows, onboarding, and benefits in one interface. Compare its current plan details against your employee count and states. Businesses with unusual payroll rules or complex international needs should evaluate specialized alternatives.

Before switching

Ask how year-to-date payroll data is imported, who corrects filing errors, how former employees access tax forms, and how quickly support responds during a blocked payroll run.

Pricing changes frequently. Verify current pricing, plan limits, contracts, integrations, and cancellation terms directly with each vendor.
